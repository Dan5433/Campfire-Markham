# The Painful Crawl
### A painful game about cave diving, but your limbs are out of control.
## Controls
* Q and E - Rotate Arms
* A and D - Rotate Legs
* Shift - Sprint
## Credits

## Inspiration
We wanted to make a softbody game, and then I discovered motors on hinge joints in Unity, and this was made.
